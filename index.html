The Challenge

Mining operations run on
knowledge that walks out the door.

When your Mine Manager retires, your senior Environmental Officer resigns, or your Shot Firer moves on — decades of site-specific expertise, regulatory relationships, and operational workarounds leave with them. New hires repeat costly mistakes. Compliance gaps emerge silently. Regulatory submissions are missed. Production suffers.

KROS captures that knowledge in structured Skills.md modules — and makes it instantly accessible to every team member, at any hour, in plain language.

01
Knowledge Retention
Structured exit interviews capture 30 years of expertise before staff leave. Skills.md modules preserve site-specific, regulatory, and operational knowledge permanently.
02
Regulatory Compliance
53 modules cover DOE, DOSH, JMG, Mines Dept, Immigration, and LHDN obligations — with specific Act sections, deadlines, form numbers, and submission workflows.
03
Instant Expert Access
Ask KROS anything in plain English or Bahasa Malaysia. Get procedure steps, royalty calculations, blast designs, or HR precedents — in seconds, not days.
04
Cost Intelligence
DeepSeek V3 reasoning costs 85% less than Claude Opus. At 150 queries/day, KROS costs USD 55–80/month vs USD 850/month for an all-Claude system.
05
Emergency Response
Emergency queries bypass DeepSeek and receive instant Claude response. Misfires, slope collapses, chemical spills — step-by-step guidance in under 3 seconds.
06
Malaysia-Specific
Built from the ground up for Malaysian mining. Correct EPF/SOCSO rates, state royalty schedules, PLKS procedures, JMG forms, DOE Standard A/B limits, and CIDB compliance.
